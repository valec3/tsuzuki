## Brand & Style

The design system embodies Victorian occultism intertwined with cosmic eldritch mystery, evoking the sensation of transcribing grimoires and peering beyond the gray fog. Built for esoteric media archiving and personal trajectory tracking, the interface balances 19th-century antiquarian artifact sensibilities with uncompromising modern precision.

The visual direction converges **Tactile Antiquarian** craftsmanship and **Cosmic Ethereal Glassmorphism**. Key visual motifs include:
- Polished obsidian tarot slabs functioning as surface vessels.
- Chiseled antique brass rules, esoteric constellation nodes, and subtle runic dividers.
- Ethereal gray mist gradients and cosmic fog underlays that recede into an infinite midnight abyss.
- Tactile wax seals, occult glyph indicators, and deep crimson ink accents that denote ritual progression and tracking milestones.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional contrast between historic literary authority and esoteric technical codification.

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys the weight of sealed volumes, ancient pacts, and Victorian titles. Use sentence case or small caps for titles; avoid aggressive full-uppercase for extended text.
- **Body & Editorial (Outfit):** Highly legible, balanced geometric sans-serif that ensures effortless catalog navigation, long reviews, synopsis parsing, and ritual note-taking without ocular fatigue against deep dark backgrounds.
- **Metadata & Numerals (JetBrains Mono):** Applied strictly to chapter tracking, sequence numbers, timestamps, status ratings, and arcane coordinates. The monospaced rhythm mirrors typewritten inventory manifests and mechanical astrolabes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system that mimics astronomical charts and grand study archives.

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, max layout container width of 1440px with 48px outer margins. Supports asymmetric multi-pane arrangements (e.g., 3-column occult index nav, 6-column main grimoire stream, 3-column astrolabe inspector).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, 16px gutters, 32px margins. Secondary sidebars collapse into slide-out drawer grimoires.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 12px gutters, 16px margins. Media cards snap into a vertical stack of tarot slabs or horizontal paginated carousels.

Strict 4px/8px micro-rhythm governs vertical spacing. Esoteric section dividers employ razor-thin 1px brass rules terminating in central diamond pips (`◆`) instead of arbitrary thick white space.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this design system is architectural and atmospheric, relying on layered cosmic fog, translucent obsidian slabs, and delicate brass filigree borders rather than generic muddy drop shadows.

- **Layer 0 (Cosmic Void Base):** Deep `#0a0d12` tinted with an ultra-subtle radial noise layer and an ethereal gray mist vignette (`radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% 0%, rgba(100,116,139,0.12) 0%, transparent 70%)`).
- **Layer 1 (Recessed Well):** Inset surfaces `#080a0f` with `box-shadow: inset 0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`. Used for progress tracks, log feeds, and code terminals.
- **Layer 2 (Obsidian Tarot Slabs):** Surface `#10141d` at 85% opacity paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Bordered with `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.22)`.
- **Layer 3 (Floating Grimoire Cards & Modals):** Surface `#161b26` at 92% opacity, framed by double-lined borders (`1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.45)` with an inner offset highlight). Ambient depth is achieved via `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75), 0 0 24px -4px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`.
- **Eldritch Resonance (Focus & Active States):** Interactive elements emit a faint spectral cyan aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(0, 245, 212, 0.25)`) or a blood moon pulse (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(230, 57, 70, 0.3)`).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined, disciplined soft-chiseled contour (`roundedness: 1`). Elements favor precision geometric cuts reminiscent of trimmed leather bookbinding, cut gemstone seals, and engraved brass plates:

- Standard controls and cards use subtle `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii, preserving structural gravitas.
- Tarot cards and media slabs use `0.5rem` (8px) corner radii.
- Circular geometry is reserved strictly for wax seal badges, astronomical dials, glyph buttons, and user sigils.
- Never use pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) geometry for cards, tabs, or text inputs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Alchemical Gold):** Solid antique gold fill (`#c5a059`) with ink-black text (`#0a0d12`), font `JetBrains Mono` uppercase with tracking. Hover triggers an eldritch gold glow and border shift to `#dfb76c`.
- **Secondary (Obsidian & Brass):** Background `#161b26`, border `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)`, text `#f1f5f9`. On hover, the border shifts to `#00f5d4` with an inner fog blur.
- **Destructive (Blood Rite):** Deep crimson background (`#9e2a2b`), border `1px solid rgba(230, 57, 70, 0.5)`, text `#f1f5f9`. Hover activates a blood-moon glow (`#e63946`).

### Wax Stamp Status Badges
- Circular or scalloped emblem badges mimicking real melted wax seals.
- Completed / Mastered: Crimson seal (`#9e2a2b`) with embossed gold sigil.
- Reading / Active: Obsidian seal with pulsating eldritch cyan center dot (`#00f5d4`).
- On Hold / Sealed: Weathered charcoal seal with muted silver filigree.

### Obsidian Tarot Slabs (Media Cards)
- Vertical aspect ratio cards (2:3 or 16:9 cinematic variants).
- Framed with an ornate 1px brass border (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)`) and micro-etched corner tick marks.
- Media cover art overlaid with a bottom-to-top gradient fading into `#10141d`.
- Bottom rail displays title in `Playfair Display` and chapter progress in `JetBrains Mono`.

### Delicate Filigree Toggles
- Track: Recessed dark charcoal canal (`#080a0f`) with a fine antique brass border.
- Thumb: Chiseled metallic brass coin stamped with a centered occult crosshair or solar glyph. Active state illuminates thumb with an eldritch cyan backlight.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background: Translucent midnight slate (`rgba(16, 20, 29, 0.75)`) with an etched bottom brass rule (`border-b border-[#c5a059]/40`).
- Text: `#f1f5f9` in `Outfit`; placeholder in `#94a3b8` italic serif.
- Focus: Bottom border glows in eldritch cyan (`#00f5d4`) with a soft mist baseline glow.

### Astrolabe Progress Bar
- Dual-layer ritual tracker: background rail is recessed abyss charcoal, while the filled segment utilizes a linear gradient from antique gold (`#c5a059`) to eldritch cyan (`#00f5d4`), crowned by a diamond cursor pip.
